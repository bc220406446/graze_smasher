Privacy & Data Policy

Clear and simple details on what information we collect, how reviews are scored, and how your privacy is protected.

1. About Graze Smasher

Graze Smasher is a tool created to help shoppers spot fake, copied, or misleading product reviews. It reads the wording of reviews to see whether they look genuine or generated by bots and spammers.

We believe in honesty, fairness, and keeping things simple. We do not sell your personal details, and we do not track your activity across the web.

2. What Information We Collect

Here is a simple summary of the data we receive and why:

Information Why We Need It How It Is Stored
Review Text & Star Rating Used only to check if the review is genuine or fake and to calculate the product's trust score. Saved with the product so shoppers can view it.
Internet Address (IP) We do not save your IP address when you submit reviews, and we never share it with outside websites. Never saved in review records and removed from all public API feeds.
Account Details (Name, Email, Password) Used only to let you log in securely and manage your saved products or reviews. Passwords are kept strictly protected with standard security scrambles. We never store plain text passwords.
Review Scores Shows the percentage likelihood that a review is genuine. Displayed alongside the review to help buyers make informed choices.
3. Ethical Data Privacy & Zero IP Sharing

Your personal privacy is always respected:

  • Review authenticity scoring is 100% text-based: The score is calculated only from the words in the review and how they match the star rating.
  • Customer IP addresses are never stored with your reviews.
  • Outside shopping websites that connect to our service never receive any personal reviewer information.
4. Multi-Model ML Ensemble & Fair Scoring

Our system checks reviews fairly using three combined tests:

  • Word Choice & Patterns (50%): Checks whether the wording matches natural customer writing or repetitive spam copy.
  • Review Structure (30%): Checks sentence length, punctuation, and capital letters.
  • Tone & Star Consistency (20%): Checks if positive words match high star ratings, or if negative words clash with 5-star ratings.
  • Simple Results: Every review gets a clear score from 0% to 100% and is simply labeled as Genuine or Fake.
5. Security & Your Rights
  • Password Safety: Your password is fully protected using standard industry security so no one can read it.
  • Your Account: You can edit your profile information or delete your account at any time.
  • Help & Questions: If you have any questions or notice an incorrect review score, you can flag the product or contact an administrator.
