HOW IT WORKS

From Raw Reviews to Machine Learning Verdicts

Our automated verification pipeline inspects consumer feedback through four deterministic computational phases.

1 Phase 01
Multi-Platform Ingestion

Collects product title, catalog category, raw text, star rating, verified purchaser badge, submission timestamp, and client IP metadata across marketplaces.

2 Phase 02
NLP Feature Vectorization

Tokenizes text into TF-IDF unigram and bigram matrices, computes TextBlob polarity (-1.0 to +1.0), evaluates subjectivity, and calculates linguistic entropy.

3 Phase 03
Dual-Model Inference

Evaluates vectorized data concurrently through LinearSVC hyperplane separation and J-48 (C4.5) entropy decision trees with IP velocity checks.

4 Phase 04
Scoring & Pure Rating

Generates a 0–100% Authenticity Score, tags the review as Genuine,Want to test custom review copy live or Fake, updates product community trust, and dispatches alerts.
